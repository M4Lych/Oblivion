# Privacy

Oblivion currently works without an account. The display name, preferences, imported worlds, and imported skins are stored in Oblivion's app-private storage. The optional analytics preference is off by default; no telemetry endpoint is currently configured and the app currently sends no analytics.

Diagnostic logs are local. The report-preview action does not upload them because a protected endpoint is not configured. The app does not collect passwords, tokens, precise location, private messages, or unrelated device files.

Deleting app data removes local profiles, imports, and logs. Account creation/deletion and server-side retention policies will be documented before those services are enabled.
